# ADR 0003: Open-source licence selection pending

Status: awaiting owner decision.

The inspected repository had no commits, licence file or licensing decision.
This foundation does not select a licence on the owner's behalf. The project
intends to be open source, but the repository currently supplies no open-source
licence grant. The workspace packages remain private and unpublished.

A permissive approach, such as MIT or Apache-2.0, generally lowers barriers to
reuse and incorporation into proprietary products. Apache-2.0 additionally
includes explicit patent provisions and notice obligations; the exact licence
text matters.

A copyleft approach, such as GPL-3.0, generally requires covered distributed
modifications or combined works to remain under compatible terms. AGPL-3.0 adds
network-interaction source obligations in relevant circumstances. These
approaches can protect downstream access to improvements while affecting
adoption and compatibility choices.

The owner should choose the desired reuse and reciprocity model, confirm
authority to license contributions and dependencies, and then add the actual
chosen licence text and update README/package metadata. Consider qualified
advice for the intended commercial model. Optional paid implementation services
can coexist with either approach; they do not determine the licence
automatically.

No contributor licence agreement or rights assignment is introduced by this
scaffold.
